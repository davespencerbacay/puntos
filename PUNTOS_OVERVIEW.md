# Puntos — Project Goals & Product Vision

## 1. Project Overview

**Puntos** is a multi-tenant, SaaS-based points and loyalty management platform designed for small, medium, and large businesses.

The platform enables businesses to establish their own loyalty programs, reward customers for purchases and other activities, manage point transactions, and encourage repeat customer engagement through configurable points, cashback, and redemption systems.

Puntos aims to simplify customer loyalty management by providing businesses with a centralized platform where they can manage members, configure reward rules, monitor transactions, and analyze loyalty program performance.

The ultimate goal is to make loyalty and rewards management accessible to businesses of all sizes without requiring them to build their own loyalty infrastructure.

## 2. Product Vision

To become a flexible and scalable loyalty platform that connects businesses and customers through a unified points and rewards ecosystem.

Puntos should make it easy for businesses to reward customer engagement while allowing customers to earn, accumulate, track, and redeem points.

The platform should prioritize:

- Simplicity and ease of use.
- Flexible loyalty configurations.
- Transparent points transactions.
- Secure and reliable operations.
- Scalable multi-tenant architecture.
- Consistent user experience.
- Business-driven reward management.

## 3. Primary Objectives

### 3.1 Business Objectives

1. Provide businesses with an affordable loyalty management platform.
2. Allow businesses to create and manage customized points programs.
3. Increase customer retention and repeat purchases.
4. Support different business types and industries.
5. Generate recurring revenue through business subscriptions.
6. Provide businesses with actionable loyalty analytics.
7. Build a platform that can scale from small businesses to enterprise organizations.

### 3.2 Customer Objectives

1. Allow customers to register and maintain a loyalty account.
2. Earn points through qualifying transactions.
3. Receive cashback or additional points based on configurable rules.
4. Track current points balances.
5. View complete transaction histories.
6. Discover and redeem available rewards.
7. Participate in loyalty programs from different merchants where supported.

## 4. Core Business Concept

Puntos operates as a platform connecting merchants and members.

### Merchants

Merchants are businesses subscribed to or using Puntos to manage their loyalty programs.

Examples:
- Restaurants.
- Coffee shops.
- Retail stores.
- Supermarkets.
- Fitness centers.
- Online stores.
- Service providers.
- Enterprise organizations.

Merchants should be able to configure their own loyalty rules, manage members, monitor transactions, and define rewards.

### Members

Members are customers who participate in merchant loyalty programs.

They should be able to:
- Register and manage their accounts.
- Earn points.
- View points balances.
- Track transaction histories.
- Redeem points for rewards.
- Participate in eligible merchant promotions.

### Platform Administrators

Platform administrators manage the overall Puntos ecosystem.

Responsibilities include:
- Merchant management.
- Subscription management.
- Platform configuration.
- Monitoring transactions.
- User and access management.
- System-wide analytics.
- Security and audit monitoring.
